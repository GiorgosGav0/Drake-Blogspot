Drake’s Discography

Take Care (2011) — A defining album blending rap and R&B with hits like “Marvins Room.”

Nothing Was The Same (2013) — A classic record with some of his greatest performances.

More Life (2017) — One of his most successful records with hits like "Passionfruit".

Scorpion (2018) — His most record-breaking album with hits like "God's Plan" and "Nice for What".

Certified Lover Boy (2021) His most awaited album, a perfect mix of rap and singing performances.

For All The Dogs (2024) — His latest solo album.
